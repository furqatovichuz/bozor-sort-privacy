English

This policy explains what information the Bozor Sort mobile game ("the app", "we") handles. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no social features.

Information stored on your device

  • Game progress, coins, stars, boosters, settings and purchases are saved only on your device. We do not receive them.
  • The year of birth you choose on the age screen is saved only on your device. It is used to choose a suitable advertising treatment, as described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To deliver, measure and protect ads, Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic data.

  • Players under 13 receive child-directed ad requests: no personalised ads and only general-audience (G) content.
  • Players aged 13 to 15 receive age-appropriate ad treatment.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before personalised ads are used. You can change it any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment card or billing details. The app keeps the purchase token on your device only, to deliver each purchase exactly once.

Analytics

The app does not use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

The game is made for a general audience, including children. We follow the Google Play Families Policy and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents or guardians can contact us with any question.

Keeping and deleting data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it saved on your device. For data that Google collects for advertising, see the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Реклама (Google AdMob)

Приложение показывает рекламу Google AdMob — сервиса Google LLC. Для показа, измерения и защиты рекламы Google может собирать идентификаторы устройства (например, рекламный ID), IP-адрес и приблизительное местоположение на его основе, сведения об устройстве и приложении, взаимодействие с рекламой и диагностические данные.

  • Игрокам 13–15 лет применяется режим рекламы, соответствующий возрасту.
  • В Европейской экономической зоне, Великобритании и Швейцарии форма согласия Google спрашивает ваш выбор перед показом персонализированной рекламы. Изменить его можно в любой момент: Настройки → Настройки рекламы.
  • Рекламный ID можно сбросить или удалить в настройках устройства.

Подробнее: как Google использует данные приложений-партнёров и Политика конфиденциальности Google.
